OUR PHILOSOPHY

Honest Wood, Intentional Living

At Anti, we intentionally design against disposable fast-furniture trends. Every timber plank is selected by hand for its grain orientation, kiln-dried to balanced moisture content, and jointed using classic Japanese and Scandinavian mortise-and-tenon methods.

  • Zero Synthetic Foams: Natural latex cushions wrapped in virgin organic wool batting.
  • Breathable Finishes: Hand-applied plant-based oils that highlight natural wood grain rather than plastic polyurethane coats.
  • Custom Alterations: Request custom millimeters or bespoke wood stains directly via Telegram.
